# How much do you pay for government services in the Netherlands in 2025?

This calculator shows how much you payed for each of the services provided to you by the government of the Netherlands. In other words, it tells you how your tax money was spent and how much it has cost you personally based on your income. 

_Privacy notice_: All calculations are done inside your web browser on your own device. No information leaves your device or a shared with anyone.
